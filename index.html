FarmChain: Transparent Agriculture Tracking

Agriculture supply chains often lack transparency, leading to mistrust and inefficiencies. FarmChain leverages blockchain-like traceability concepts to enable farmers to register produce, distributors to verify quality, and consumers to trace origin using QR-like codes.

Farmer

Register your produce so it can be traced and sold transparently.

Distributor

Verify received produce for quality and update status.

Consumer

Trace the produce origin using QR-like codes (simulated).
